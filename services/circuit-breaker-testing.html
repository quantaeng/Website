Circuit Breaker Testing

Electrical testing, verification and commissioning activities

Scope of Service

Quanta Engineering Solutions provides structured field testing and commissioning support for circuit breaker testing, with emphasis on accurate measurements, functional verification, safety and complete documentation.

Testing & Commissioning Activities

  • Insulation Resistance
  • Timing Tests (SF6)
  • CRM & DCRM Testing
  • Coil Current Testing
  • Coil IR Testing
  • Motor Resistance & IR
  • Local/Remote Operation
  • Contact Resistance

Typical Deliverables

  • Equipment-wise test reports
  • Recorded test values and observations
  • Commissioning check sheets
  • Functional verification records
  • Punch-point observations
  • Final documentation for handover
